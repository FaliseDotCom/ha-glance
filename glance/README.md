# Glance

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield]
![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

A self-hosted dashboard for your feeds and services, with a no-code editor, running on your
Home Assistant server.

[Glance](https://github.com/glanceapp/glance) puts RSS feeds, weather, calendars, releases,
bookmarks, service monitors and much more on one fast page. This app bundles it with the
[Glance Editor](https://github.com/gcarstoiu/glance-ui), so you build that page in your
browser instead of writing `glance.yml` by hand.

- Pages, columns and every Glance widget, with drag and drop.
- Community widgets, themes, backgrounds and an icon finder.
- A private preview before anything goes live, and a version for every save.
- Bring your current Glance: upload a `.zip` of its folder.
- Photos from the Home Assistant media folder as backgrounds.
- Keeps the dashboard in Home Assistant backups.

See the **Documentation** tab for installation and configuration.
